Our home galaxy: the Milky Way

The Milky Way is honestly mind-blowing. It's a barred spiral galaxy containing somewhere between 100 and 400 billion stars — and our Sun is just one of them, sitting about 26,000 light-years from the centre.

The galaxy is roughly 100,000 light-years across and about 1,000 light-years thick at the disc. It takes our solar system around 225 million years to complete one orbit around the galactic centre. That's called a "cosmic year" — we've only done about 20 of them since Earth formed!

At BigPlanetarium, we believe the best way to understand just how huge this all is… is to come and see it for yourself in our dome theatre. Words and numbers only go so far.

The Red Planet. Home to the tallest volcano in the solar system and possibly ancient rivers. Future humans might live here — wild, right?
